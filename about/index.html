About Us

About Plantation Express Plumbing

We are a plumbing dispatch office on West Broward Boulevard. We answer the phone at any hour and send a licensed plumber.

Call (954) 697-4920See all services

What we do

Plantation Express Plumbing takes calls from homeowners, landlords and businesses in Plantation and four nearby towns. When you call, someone listens, asks what is happening and sends a plumber who can handle the problem.

We cover emergencies at any hour and schedule planned work for the next open day. Our services range from drain cleaning to repiping, and each one has its own page with plain-language detail.

How we work

You hear the price before work begins. We explain what we find. We tell you when a repair is enough and when replacement makes more sense. Where a permit is needed, as with water heaters, we handle it as the registered contractor.

Our plumbers are licensed and insured. If you want to verify any plumber's license in Florida, you can use the state's licensing lookup on the Department of Business and Professional Regulation website.

Our location

Our office is at 8211 W Broward Blvd, Plantation, FL 33324. We cover Plantation, Sunrise, Davie, Lauderhill and Tamarac. You can reach us any time at (954) 697-4920 or at contact@expressplumberplantation.com.

Who we serve

Homeowners call us when something breaks. Landlords and property managers call when a tenant reports a leak or a backup. Business owners call when a restroom or a kitchen drain stops. We treat each call the same way, with a clear explanation of what we found and what it will take to fix it.

We also take planned work. If you are remodeling, adding a bathroom or replacing aging pipes, tell us what you have in mind and we explain the steps, the permits and the timing.

Why local knowledge matters

Plantation grew quickly from the 1970s to the 1990s, so many homes share the same pipe materials and layouts. Knowing that history helps us diagnose a problem faster and recommend a repair that suits the house.

The city's utility draws groundwater from the Biscayne aquifer and treats it with membrane softening, and the city publishes sewer and permit information that we use on every job.

How a call becomes a visit

Calls start with questions. Where is the water, can you reach the shutoff, is anyone in danger and how long has it been going on. Those answers tell dispatch which truck to send and which tools it needs, so the plumber arrives ready for the real problem instead of a guess.

Once the plumber is at the door, the order stays the same. Look at the problem, explain what was found, give the price and wait for your go-ahead. Work starts after you agree, and any change in scope gets a second conversation before it happens.

Jobs we take

Our work falls into a few families. Water heaters cover repair, replacement and tankless units. Drains and sewers run from drain cleaning and hydro jetting to sewer line repair. Water supply problems include leak detection, slab leaks and burst pipes.

Fixtures and installs include toilets, faucets and disposals, and larger projects include repiping and commercial work. The services page lists all twenty.

Permits and codes

Plumbing in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, and many jobs, water heater swaps among them, need a permit. The City of Plantation runs a same-day permit program for existing single-family homes with a plumbing water heater checklist, and the contractor has to be registered in the city system.

We tell you early whether your job needs a permit, because it affects timing. Ask on the phone and we explain what applies to your address before a plumber is scheduled.

Hurricane season and the pipes

South Florida plumbing gets tested every storm season. Heavy rain can overload sewer lines, a power outage can stop lift stations and a loose water heater or a cracked supply line can fail when a house sits closed up for days. We hear about these problems in clusters, so dispatch plans for busy weeks.

Before a storm, know where your main water shutoff is and how to turn it. After one, look for wet ceilings, slow drains and a meter that keeps moving when everything is off. Call us if you find any of them, and we tell you what can wait and what cannot.

What we do not do

We do not quote from a phone description and promise it will never change. Pipes hide things, and a good plumber says so. We also do not push replacement when a repair will hold. If we think a pipe has years left, we say that and repair it.

Some jobs need other trades, such as electricians for a failed circuit or restoration crews for water-damaged drywall. When that happens we say so and point you toward the right kind of help.
