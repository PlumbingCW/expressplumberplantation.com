A sewer line fails when a pipe cracks, a joint separates, roots push in or a section sags and holds water. Signs include repeat backups, gurgling in several fixtures, wet ground over the line and a sewage smell outdoors.

Our approach is to confirm the problem on camera, show you what we saw and then lay out repair options, from a spot repair to full replacement.

Sewage coming up in the house

Stop using water in the house, including toilets, showers and the washing machine. Keep children and pets away from standing wastewater, and open windows to ventilate. If sewage is soaking carpet or drywall, tell us on the phone so we can plan the visit and you can start calling your insurer.

We dispatch at any hour. Backups are not a wait-and-see problem.

Finding where the line is broken

A camera pushed from a cleanout travels the whole line and records the cracks, offsets and root masses. We note the distance from the access point and use a locator to mark the spot at ground level. That tells us how deep the pipe is and how big the dig will be.

Our page on sewer camera inspection explains the process in more detail.

Repair paths for a damaged line

Spot repair

When damage is limited to one section, we excavate and replace that stretch. It is the most direct fix when the rest of the pipe is sound.

Pipe lining

A resin-saturated liner is pulled through and cured inside the existing pipe. It can avoid trenching in a yard or under a driveway, though it needs a pipe that is still mostly intact.

Full replacement

A badly deteriorated or collapsed line may need to be replaced end to end. We explain what that means for landscaping, paving and timing before you decide.

City mains and your house line

Plantation maintains about 197 miles of gravity sewer main, 57 miles of force main and 125 lift stations. Your house line connects to that network, and where responsibility changes depends on the property. The camera finding helps decide whether the failure is on your side or in the city main, and we tell you what we found so you can contact the right party.

Before any excavation, we confirm utility locations through the Sunshine 811 service.

What the finished repair looks like

After the repair, we camera the line again to confirm the new section is aligned and watertight. We backfill the trench, compact the soil and restore the surface as agreed. You receive a description of the work and the footage, which is helpful for your records and for any insurance conversation.

We also give you a simple maintenance note. Keep grease out of the line, watch for roots and call if you see early warning signs again.

Roots and trees near the line

Roots seek water and enter through tiny joints. A cleaned pipe fills with roots again if the joint stays open. Repair or lining closes the entry. If a large tree is near the line, we talk with you about the options, including cutting roots, repairing the pipe and managing the tree.

Codes and permits for this work

Plumbing in Florida is governed by the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). In practice, sewer repairs, especially replacements, can need a permit and an inspection. If you want to know what applies to your address, ask the dispatcher and we explain it before the visit.

What affects sewer line repair cost

Sewer repair spans a wide range because the situation does, and no honest quote exists before the camera run.

Depth and location
A shallow pipe in a lawn is cheaper to reach than a deep one under a slab or driveway.
Length of damage
A single break differs from a stretch of failing pipe.
Method
Spot repair, lining and replacement each have different labor and material needs.
Restoration
Landscaping, concrete and sod repair after digging are part of the plan.
