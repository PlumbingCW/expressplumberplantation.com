What to do when a leak starts in Davie

The first move is the same everywhere. Stop the water. On a larger lot, that may mean finding the meter box near the road. If the leak is in the yard, wet turf and a spongy patch point to a buried line, and we locate it before digging.

Davie is also a place where utilities overlap. Parts of Davie are served by the town and parts by Sunrise Utilities, so we help you work out whom to call if the break is on the utility side.

Before we arrive: what to do while you wait

Close the water at the meter box

On larger Davie lots the meter can sit at the road. Turn the valve a quarter turn clockwise with a meter key or large pliers.

Drop the breakers

Cut power to the garage, laundry and bathrooms first, because those are the rooms where wet floors and outlets meet.

Move what you can

Get furniture, boxes and tools up off the floor, and check the garage if the heater lives there.

Larger lots, longer pipes

Many properties in Davie and nearby Plantation Acres have more distance between the meter, the house and outbuildings. A leak in that run can waste water for weeks because nothing shows inside the house. Look for lush patches of grass, a muddy spot that never dries or a bill that has jumped.

Irrigation, pool equipment and barn or guest house connections add more joints that can leak. When you call, tell us what is connected to the main line so we can look at every branch.

Roots, trees and old laterals

Large trees are a feature of many Davie properties, and their roots find sewer laterals. A line that backs up every few months, or a toilet that gurgles during heavy rain, may be telling you roots have entered. A camera shows the answer.
