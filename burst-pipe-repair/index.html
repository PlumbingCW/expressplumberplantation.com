Pipes burst from age, corrosion, bad fittings, pressure surges and impact during remodels. South Florida rarely freezes, so burst pipes here are usually a failure of the pipe itself or a connection that has let go.

What matters first is stopping the water. Everything after that, drying, repair and restoration, goes smoothly only if the water stops quickly.

Stop the flood in the next two minutes

  1. Find the main shutoff valve and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Open a lower faucet to drain the lines and relieve pressure.
  3. Turn off power to flooded rooms at the breaker if you can reach it safely.
  4. Call us with the location of the break and what the pipe looks like.

Do not touch electrical items in standing water. If the water is near outlets or an electrical panel, stay out and call an electrician as well.

What we do when we arrive

We confirm the water is off, trace the failed section and cut out the damaged pipe. We replace it with new material and test the line under pressure before restoring flow. If the break is a symptom of widespread corrosion, we say so instead of leaving you with the next failure.

Water in the building can affect other trades, so we explain what to tell your insurance adjuster and any drying contractor.

Why pipes burst in local homes

Older galvanized pipes corrode from the inside and thin out. Polybutylene, used in many homes built through the mid-1990s, becomes brittle and is known for failing at fittings. Copper can develop pinholes. Poorly secured pipes also vibrate and wear.

If your home has one of these materials, ask about repiping before a second failure happens.

Dealing with water damage after the repair

Wet drywall and insulation need to dry quickly. Fans and dehumidifiers help, and a restoration company can extract water and monitor moisture. Mold can begin to grow if materials stay damp for long. Keep photos of everything for the claim.

Preparing for the plumber

Clear a path to the main valve, the water heater and the damaged area. Move furniture away from wet walls. If a ceiling is bulging with water, a small hole made with a screwdriver can let it drain into a bucket, and that often saves the ceiling. Stand to one side as you do it.

Gather insurance details. Photograph the break, the wet areas and anything damaged before cleanup, because adjusters ask for it.

Materials we use for the repair

We match the repair to the existing pipe where we can. Copper sections are repaired with copper and soldered or press-fit joints. PEX is repaired with approved fittings. Galvanized steel is usually replaced with a modern material instead of patched, because corrosion elsewhere in the pipe will cause another failure.

We test the repaired section under pressure before we restore full flow.

Codes and permits for this work

Plumbing in Florida is governed by the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). In practice, replacement pipe and fittings must be approved for potable water under the plumbing code. If you want to know what applies to your address, ask the dispatcher and we explain it before the visit.

What affects burst pipe repair cost

A split in a visible copper line costs far less than one behind tile or in a ceiling.

Pipe material and size
Copper, PEX, galvanized and CPVC each need different fittings and methods.
