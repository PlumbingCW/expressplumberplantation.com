The City of Plantation's neighborhood planning document names a Lago Mar area near the meeting point of Flamingo Road, Broward Boulevard and Interstate 595. The city describes housing there as excellent.

That area includes upscale single-family homes, three-story multifamily buildings and a mobile home park. Local guides also describe Lago Mar as a gated community built around a private golf course.

Where Lago Mar sits

Plantation grew westward in the 1970s through the 1990s, and the city's history says its boundaries reached out to 136th Avenue around Lago Mar. Listing addresses for the area use ZIP code 33325 and avenues in the NW 129th to 131st range.

Because the city's planning document and local real estate guides describe the area a little differently, we use the Flamingo Road, Broward Boulevard and I-595 corner as the anchor rather than a firm boundary.

Plumbing services Lago Mar homes ask about

Larger homes often run a bigger water heater and more fixtures. Water heater repair and water heater replacement come up often, along with faucet repair and disposal repair.

For slow or backed-up lines we offer drain cleaning. If a leak is hidden in a wall or slab, slab leak repair starts with locating the leak before any opening is made.

Signs a plumber should look at your pipes

A water bill that climbs without a change in habits, a faint sound of running water at night and damp patches on walls all deserve attention. So do slow drains that return a few days after cleaning.

These signs do not prove a failing pipe. They are reasons to book a visit so a plumber can check before a small leak becomes damage.

A note on water heaters in larger homes

Larger homes often use bigger tanks or more than one heater. A heater that makes noise, leaks at the base or takes longer to heat is worth a look before it fails completely.

We check the unit, the venting or electrical supply and the relief valve line. Then we explain whether repair or replacement makes sense.
