Home/Contact
Call Anytime

Contact Plantation Express Plumbing

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. We answer 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Call for plumbing help

Call (954) 697-4920 and tell us what is happening. For a burst pipe, a backed-up drain or a failed water heater, the call is the first step. We tell you what to shut off and send a plumber.

For planned work, such as an installation or a camera inspection, ask for the next available day and we confirm a window.

What to have ready

  • Your address, the street and any gate or building details.
  • What is happening, where and since when.
  • Whether water is still running and if you can reach the main shutoff.
  • Any photos of the damage or the unit's data plate.

Business hours

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Our office address is for correspondence and does not take walk-in service calls, so please phone before you visit.

After you call

Dispatch confirms your address and the problem and gives you an arrival window. The plumber calls when they are on the way. On arrival, they look at the problem, explain what they found and give you a price before starting.

Our office

Plantation Express Plumbing, 8211 W Broward Blvd, Plantation, FL 33324. Email contact@expressplumberplantation.com for non-urgent questions. For anything urgent, please phone.
