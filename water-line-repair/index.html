Wet ground in the yard, no water at the tap or a meter that will not stop spinning can all point to the line between the meter and the house.

The water service line carries water from the city meter to your home. If it leaks, you see soggy turf, a sinkhole or a sudden drop in pressure, and your bill goes up for water you never used.

We find the break, expose the pipe and repair or replace it.

Signs the line is leaking

  • A wet or sinking patch of lawn with no irrigation running.
  • Low pressure throughout the house.
  • A meter that keeps moving with the house shut off.
  • A sound of running water near the meter box or driveway.
  • A sudden jump in your bill.

If water is spilling in the yard

Close the main valve at the house. If the line is still flowing, the break may be on the city side of the meter. Call Plantation Utilities in that case. If the break is on your side, call us.

Mark the wet area and avoid driving a vehicle over it. Soft soil over a broken line can give way.

How we repair a service line

We confirm the location with listening gear and a pipe locator, then call Sunshine 811 before we dig. We excavate, expose the pipe and repair a short section or replace a longer stretch. Modern materials such as PEX or copper fit most homes, and we choose what suits your soil and layout.

Trenchless methods can reduce disruption under a driveway or walkway when the situation allows.

Related problems we check at the same time

Low pressure can come from a failing pressure regulator or a partly closed valve, not just a broken line. If the source is inside the house, a leak detection visit covers that. If the whole home has aging pipes, see repiping for a longer-term plan.

After the repair

We flush the line, check pressure at the house and confirm the meter is still when everything is off. We backfill and compact the trench, then restore the surface. If you have an irrigation system tied into the line, we check those connections as well.

Keep an eye on the lawn over the next week. If a soft spot returns, call us and we will recheck.

Pressure regulators and shutoffs

The pressure regulator near the meter or the house protects your plumbing from high pressure. A failing one can cause leaks that look like a line problem. We test pressure and replace the regulator when it is weak, and we confirm the main shutoff valve works so you can stop water in the future.

Prevention and early warning

Know where your meter box and main shutoff sit. Check the meter occasionally with everything off. Walk the yard after rain and look for soft spots. Keep heavy equipment off the line route when you can. A small leak found early is a much smaller job than a main that washes out the soil.

Codes and permits for this work

Plumbing in Florida is governed by the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). In practice, work on the line from the meter to the house is done to code and may need a permit. If you want to know what applies to your address, ask the dispatcher and we explain it before the visit.

What affects water line repair cost

The length of the run and what lies on top of it decide most of the cost.
