Privacy Policy

How Plantation Express Plumbing handles information when you call us or visit this website.

Information we collect

When you call us, we collect your phone number, address, the details of the problem and anything you tell the dispatcher. When you email us, we keep your message and address. Our server and hosting provider may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

This website has no forms, accounts or checkout, so we do not collect information through sign-up pages.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use it to answer your call, dispatch a plumber, give you a price, schedule a visit, follow up on the work and improve our service. We share job details with the plumber assigned to your visit. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds Google Maps. Those providers may receive your IP address and device details when the content loads, and they handle that data under their own policies.

Retention and security

We keep call and job records for as long as needed for service, accounting and legal purposes. We use reasonable measures to protect them, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@expressplumberplantation.com or call (954) 697-4920. We may update this policy, and the date of the latest change appears on this page.

Where we work around Plantation

Dispatch covers Plantation plus Sunrise, Davie, Lauderhill and Tamarac in Broward County.
