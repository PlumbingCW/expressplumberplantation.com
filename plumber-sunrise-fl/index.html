Before we arrive: what to do while you wait

Shut off the main valve

Find the shutoff near the meter or where the line enters the house and turn it clockwise. In a Sunrise condo, ask the association which riser valve serves your unit.

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for any room with standing water. If the panel sits in the wet area, stay out and tell us on the phone.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics and cardboard off the floor. Photograph the damage before you move much, because adjusters ask for it.
